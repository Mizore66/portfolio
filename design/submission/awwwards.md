## Description (draft, for the owner's approval)

**A (third person, 3 sentences):** A portfolio played as a game of chess. The line down every page is an engine's evaluation bar, and the paper side is White's share of the position: roles are seven famous games, each project stands on the square its move landed on, and the Lab publishes a chess engine Anas trained, including the match it lost. Every piece, board and light is made in code, and the engine is playable.

**B (one line):** Anas Qumhiyeh's portfolio as a chess game, where the seam between paper and black is an engine's evaluation bar, with a playable engine and the published results of a net that lost.

Every claim is from the site's own copy: the colophon ("The seam", "Made for this site", "The engine"), Roles ("Seven famous games, one per role"), the Work room and the Lab ("The learned evaluator lost to PeSTO").
