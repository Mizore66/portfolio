Checks

What was verified

text clashesNone at 1440×900 and 390×844 across 9 instants, or at 1024×768 and 360×640 in the end state.
production buildNo CSP errors; the hero hydrates under the nonce. Fast 4G: first paint 0.29 s; the opening starts at 1.2 s on a laptop and 2.3 s with the CPU slowed 4× (budget 2.5 s). JavaScript 316 KB.
frame rateMac GPU, both sizes: median 8 ms a frame, 95th percentile 16 ms.
testse2e/hero.spec.ts: the opening plays and rests on the seam; it plays once per session; reduced motion shows the end state and passes axe with one heading and one navigation; without JavaScript the name, line and links show. 54 unit tests, 9 e2e tests pass.
