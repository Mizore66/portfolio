Holds up: the hero, Roles, Other Projects, the Lab, Play, Lab 04, the role and project pages, the colophon.
Needs its own tablet composition:

- **Work:** the gallery sits small in the middle of a tall dark field; on a tablet the plinths can come forward and
  fill the width, the names beside them.
- **Lab 01:** the 20,000,000 positions' floor is a small patch with a screen of nothing above it; the camera can come
  down so the floor fills the frame.
- **Contact:** the move number, "Your move." and the email sit in the top third, the clock and links low on the dark
  side, half the screen empty between; on a tablet the links can sit beside the clock.
- **Play:** fits, but the controls are spread to the corners (opponent and side at the top, the rest at the foot); on a
  tablet they can sit together under the board.
