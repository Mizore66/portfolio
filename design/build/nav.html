Questions for the owner

Three calls the storyboard does not make

  1. No travel. Hero to Contact (and to project pages, which also rest at 55.9%) has nothing to sweep. The build lifts the old page away. The alternative: the seam dips toward the edge and comes back (about 1.4 s, one more sweep on screen).
  2. What is left. When the seam lands short of an edge, the rest of the old page rises out of its own region (hero to Lab). Keep it, or let the seam overshoot to the edge first? Overshooting breaks the "nothing overshoots" rule.
  3. The slow arrival. Returning to the hero waits about 0.3 s while its 3D stage is rebuilt. It can be removed by keeping the hero's stage alive between pages, which keeps its two WebGL contexts (and their GPU memory) alive while you are elsewhere on the site.
