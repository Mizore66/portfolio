Key frames, round 1

The combined concept from Gate 1: the Advantage seam for the hero and contact, the night gallery for work, the day hall for roles, and the search in the Lab. Frames are static and throwaway: real fonts (Archivo, JetBrains Mono), real copy from content.json, real 3D (three.js, lathe-turned pieces, physically based materials, one key light), and chess positions from the kept engine. Motion comes in Phase 3. Each check line is marked ✓ pass, ~ partial, ✗ fail.

1. Hero

Same system, three compositions. Mobile is designed separately: on phones the seam turns horizontal, like a phone-sized eval bar.

One dominant element: the name, with strong scale contrast
Typographic moment at a scale no ordinary site uses
Résumé link in the same corner on every frame
20 words or fewer (11 to 13)
Signature moment in the first 5 s: defined by motion (the seam swinging through the game), which is Phase 3
Legible without chess: the split reads as design, but not yet as "advantage" without the move label

2. Signature transition: hero to Work

The seam sweeps, and the black side is a window onto the gallery underneath, so the two states stay connected throughout.

The transition connects both states; nothing fades out and back in
No scroll-jacking: the sweep is driven by one click or a pinned scroll

3. Work index: the night gallery

Materials argue for each project: porcelain crack, aluminium, basalt with a copper trace
One number per project, with its qualifier
work-a: three equal plinths weaken scale contrast, and it has no typographic moment
Knight silhouette is still rough; Phase 4 models it properly

4. Project page: FaultLine

One dominant element in each, with different scale relationships
Claim, qualifier and move on the first screen
Cast shadow is long and hard in proj-a; soften in the next round

5. Roles: the day hall

Email, LinkedIn, GitHub and résumé all on one screen
20 words or fewer
Clock times are samples; the build shows live times

Across the whole set
