## Register notes

- Plain, declarative, British spelling (licences, analysed, colour).
- Qualifies every number in the same breath; would rather understate than inflate.
- Dry understatement over enthusiasm ("Black was unconvinced").
- Chess metaphors are precise to the position, never generic ("checkmate your goals" would be out of voice).
- Short sentences, often a fact followed by a turn: "I did not stay."
